## Rustam Rakhimov Igorevich [rusyasoft]

I am a software engineer focused on backend systems and software architecture.
Most of my day-to-day work is building and operating services at scale, and
lately a growing share of it is spent understanding how AI agents fit into real
engineering workflows. I am currently a Software Development Engineer at Amazon.

This blog is where I write up the parts of that work that seem worth keeping:
architecture decisions and the reasoning behind them, backend and distributed
systems patterns, AI-agent design, and the occasional security or algorithms
note. I try to write from real problems rather than tutorials, so the posts lean
toward trade-offs, failure modes, and "why", not just step-by-step recipes.

> The views and opinions on this blog are my own and do not represent my
> employer.

## What you will find here

- **Software architecture** - decision records, system design, and the
  trade-offs behind them.
- **Backend engineering** - services, data stores, and distributed systems.
- **AI agents** - agent state, tooling, and how agents change the way we design
  and implement software.
- **Security and algorithms** - focused notes from courses, reading, and
  practice.
